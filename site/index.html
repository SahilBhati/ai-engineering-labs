Each piece is a token with an ID. Try unbelievable, flooooded or another language. Lesson 1 explains why.

You can't tune what you can't see inside.

Most AI material either stays at the API level or dives into research math. Engineers who build and run AI systems need the middle: what each part of the model does, and what it costs in memory and time.

Every lesson starts with a small worked example and a visual you control. Change a number, watch the effect, then read why. The goal is an intuition you can reason with in a design review, not a definition to memorize.
